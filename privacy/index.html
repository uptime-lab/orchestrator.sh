Privacy

This notice covers the website orchestrator.sh. Last updated 23 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Uptime Lab Inc., 99 Wall Street #4269, New York, NY 10005, United States of America. Contact for privacy questions: info@orchestrator.sh.

What this website does not do

It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. It shows no advertising and builds no profile of you. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party. For that reason there is no cookie banner.

Visit statistics

We count page views with GoatCounter, which loads a script from gc.zgo.at and sends each page view to orchestrator.goatcounter.com. For each page view it records the page, the site you came from, and your browser, system, screen width, language and country, and keeps them as hourly and daily totals. Your IP address is used to work out the country and is not stored. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing how often the website is visited and from where (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GoatCounter describes what it stores in its privacy policy.

Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, GitHub necessarily receives your IP address and the usual request data (time, page, browser type) and may keep them in its logs for security and to operate the service. We do not have access to these logs and do not combine them with other data. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in providing the website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub describes its processing in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

When you write to us

If you send us an email, we process your address and whatever you write in order to answer you and, if you ask about a subscription, to prepare an offer. Our mailboxes are provided by Google Workspace (Google LLC). Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep the correspondence for as long as the enquiry or the resulting business relationship requires and the law demands, and delete it afterwards.

Transfers outside the European Union

We are located in the United States, and so are GitHub and Google. Data you send us is therefore processed in the United States.

Links to other sites

This website links to GitHub, to computeblade.com and to the hosted service at app.orchestrator.sh. Those sites have their own privacy terms; nothing is sent to them until you follow a link.

Your rights

Where the GDPR applies, you may ask us for access to your data, for its correction or deletion, for restriction of its processing and for a copy in a portable format, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to info@orchestrator.sh. You may also complain to the data protection authority of your country.
