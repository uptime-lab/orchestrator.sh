Uptime Orchestrator is in public beta.

Self-hosted, it is free for up to 25 devices per server, with every feature. A server that manages more than 25 devices needs a subscription. On the hosted service, 10 devices are free; more needs a subscription.

Subscriptions are not on sale yet. During the beta, we handle larger installs and enterprise customers one by one: ask us for pricing by email.

Ask about pricing

Opens your mail app. Or write to sales@orchestrator.sh with the subject “Orchestrator subscription”.

What to put in the message

  • How many devices you run now, and how many you expect to run.
  • Self-hosted or hosted: your own server, or the hosted service at app.orchestrator.sh.
  • Your company, or say that it is a personal project.
  • A contact: the name and address we should reply to.
Limit If your server sent you here

What happens when a server reaches its limit

Nothing stops

The server keeps working, with every feature on.

Current devices keep working

Every device the server already manages stays managed, with its users, software, images and recovery.

New devices wait

A device that tries to join after the limit is reached is held as pending. It stays pending until a slot is free.

Free a slot

Remove a device you no longer need, and the next pending device is admitted.

Apply a subscription

A subscription is a file for one server. The server's operator applies it on Account → Subscription, signed in as a Super Admin of the account that runs the server, and the limit of that server is raised or removed.
